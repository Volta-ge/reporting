Website Traffic (Google Analytics 4)

Sessions/Users/Conversions cover all traffic to the site (every channel, not only paid), from GA4 property 369140604. Engaged Sessions = sessions lasting 10s+, with 2+ pageviews, or with a conversion (GA4's own "real visit" filter, comparable to Meta's Outbound Clicks / Google Ads' Interactions above). Conversions = GA4 key events. Month columns and the "Last 7 days" / "Total" column are asked of GA4 as one aggregate each, exactly like GA4's own reports — so Users counts a person once per period (not once per day) and Sessions can differ slightly from the sum of the daily cells (a session that straddles midnight shows up on both days). Engaged Sessions / Engagement rate: GA4 finalizes engagement about 2 days late (a just-ended day can show a tenth of its real value), so the newest 2 days are shown as – and the current month, "Last 7 days" and "Total" cells count only the fully processed days (through 2026-10-23).

What each metric counts
Meta Ads
Spend
Total amount spent, in the account's own currency.
Impressions
How many times an ad was shown on screen. The same person seeing it twice counts as 2.
Clicks (all)
Every tap anywhere on the ad unit — likes, comments, shares, photo-expand, page-name tap — not just clicks that leave Facebook/Instagram for the site.
Outbound Clicks
Only the clicks that actually took someone to the website. The metric that corresponds to a real site visit.
CTR (outbound)
Outbound Clicks ÷ Impressions — the share of ad views that sent someone to the site.
CPC (outbound)
Spend ÷ Outbound Clicks — the real cost per site visit driven.
Outbound Clicks per $1
Outbound Clicks ÷ Spend — the inverse of CPC: how many site visits one dollar buys.
Google Ads
Cost
Total amount spent, in the account's own currency.
Impressions
How many times an ad was shown.
Interactions
The platform's broader "main user action" count — clicks plus other engagement (e.g. swiping a Shopping ad's images).
Clicks
Clicks specifically. Usually close to Interactions; the two differ when a campaign has non-click engagement.
Conversions
Completed target actions (as defined in the Google Ads account), attributed to the campaign/day.
CTR
Clicks ÷ Impressions.
CPC
Cost ÷ Clicks.
Cost / Conversion
Cost ÷ Conversions — how much each completed conversion cost.
Clicks per $1
Clicks ÷ Cost — the inverse of CPC: how many clicks one dollar buys.
Website Traffic (GA4)
Sessions
One visit episode — starts when someone arrives, ends after 30 minutes of inactivity. One person can create several sessions in a day.
Users
Unique people, regardless of how many sessions each had.
Engaged Sessions
Sessions lasting 10 seconds or more, with 2+ pageviews, or that included a conversion — GA4's own filter for a "real" visit vs. an instant bounce.
Conversions
GA4 key events (e.g. form submits, purchases) reached during the session.
Engagement rate
Engaged Sessions ÷ Sessions.
Conversion rate
Conversions ÷ Sessions.
Combined Ad Spend
Meta Spend + Google Ads Cost for that day/month — GA4 itself has no cost data, this is pulled in from the other two tabs.
Sessions per $1 (blended)
Sessions ÷ Combined Ad Spend. Unlike Meta's/Google Ads' own per-$1 metrics, this is blended: the numerator is ALL site traffic (paid + organic + direct + referral), not just the ads' own visitors, so it is not a clean per-channel efficiency number — a rough "how far did total ad spend go" view, nothing more.

All three channels show a "raw platform count" next to its "qualified/outbound" counterpart (Meta: Clicks (all) vs Outbound Clicks; Google Ads: Interactions vs Clicks; GA4: Sessions vs Engaged Sessions) so the numbers stay comparable across tabs — e.g. why an ad platform's own click count can run far ahead of GA4 Sessions.
